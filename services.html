What We Supply

MEP Materials & Piping Products Across UAE

Aquasafe Trading is the authorized UAE distributor for globally recognized MEP brands, covering every requirement of modern construction projects.

PPR Pipe Supply UAE

We supply Polymelt PP-R and PP-RCT pipes and fittings — the gold standard for hot and cold water plumbing in UAE. Available in PN10, PN16, PN20 and PN25 pressure classes for residential and commercial MEP projects. Sizes from 20mm to 160mm, conforming to DIN 8077/8078 and ISO 15874.

Water Pump Supplier UAE

As an authorized Grundfos distributor in UAE, we supply the full range of water pumps for residential, commercial and industrial applications — including booster pumps, submersible pumps, circulation pumps and pressure booster systems. Grundfos pumps are renowned for energy efficiency and long service life.

uPVC Drainage Pipes UAE

Aquasafe Trading is an authorized Hepworth distributor in UAE, supplying uPVC and ABS drainage pipe systems conforming to BS EN 1329 and BS 4514. Our drainage range covers above-ground soil and waste systems, underground sewer pipes and civil drainage for all project types across the UAE.

Water Heater Supplier UAE

We supply Ariston water heaters across UAE — electric storage, solar thermal, and heat-pump water heaters for homes, hotels and commercial buildings. Ariston is Italy's most trusted water heating brand, combining innovation with energy efficiency for UAE's demanding climate conditions.

Ball Valves & Fittings UAE

We distribute Vybik brass ball valves, manifold splitters and plumbing fittings — engineered for reliable flow and pressure control in PPR plumbing systems. Our valve range is suitable for domestic, commercial and light industrial applications and pairs perfectly with our PPR pipe and fitting range.

Full MEP Materials Supply UAE

Beyond individual product lines, Aquasafe Trading serves as a single-source MEP materials supplier for construction projects across Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Al Ain and all UAE emirates. From bill of quantities (BOQ) support to timely on-site delivery, we are your reliable MEP trading partner. Request a quote today.
