Rheem: Trusted Water Heating Innovation

Aquasafe Trading is proud to partner with Rheem, one of the world's leading manufacturers of residential and commercial water heating solutions.

Founded in 1925, Rheem has built a century-long reputation for dependable hot water technology — from high-efficiency electric storage tanks to tankless, gas and heat-pump systems. Rheem's commitment to sustainability, innovation and integrated home comfort makes it the ideal water heating partner for UAE residential, commercial and hospitality projects.

We supply the Rheem Fury® 82V Electric Water Heater line — engineered for longer life with resistored heating elements, premium-grade anode rods and exclusive Rheemglas tank lining.

Why Choose Rheem Water Heaters?

Resistored Heating Elements

High-efficiency resistored elements provide added protection to prolong anode rod and tank life.

Exclusive Rheemglas Lining

Premium tank lining resists corrosion, extending service life in demanding water conditions.

R-Foam Insulation

Polyurethane foam insulation delivers superior heat retention and lower standby energy loss.

EverKleen™ Self-Cleaning

High-velocity spiraling water stream fights sediment build-up, saving energy and improving tank life.

High Recovery Rate

21 gallons per hour recovery at 90°F rise — reliable hot water for high-demand households.

Factory Tested Durability

150 PSI maximum working pressure, factory tested at 300 PSI with ASME-rated temperature and pressure valve.

Product Highlights

  • Capacities: 30, 40, 50, 65, 80 and 120 gallon models
  • Voltage: 240V AC single phase (415V & 480V three phase available)
  • Elements: Double and single element models — stainless steel or Incoloy
  • First Hour Rating: 48–99 GPH depending on capacity
  • Recovery: 21 GPH at 90°F temperature rise
  • Insulation: 1.5"–2.125" R-Foam polyurethane foam
  • Included: Brass drain valve, T&P relief valve, heat traps
  • Safety: Over-temperature protector and automatic thermostat
