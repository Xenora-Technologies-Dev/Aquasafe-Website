Frequently Asked Questions – uPVC Drainage UAE

Your questions about Hepworth uPVC drainage pipe selection and supply in the UAE answered.

Hepworth uPVC drainage pipes conform to BS EN 1329 for above-ground soil and waste discharge systems, BS 4514 for underground drainage, and DIN 8061/8062. They carry BSI Kitemark certification, making them fully compliant with UAE construction authority specifications and international quality requirements. Also explore our PPR plumbing systems.

uPVC (Unplasticized Polyvinyl Chloride) pipes are used primarily for underground drainage and soil stacks. ABS (Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene) waste pipes are lighter, more impact-resistant and commonly used for above-ground waste discharge from sinks, basins and baths. Hepworth supplies both systems and Aquasafe Trading stocks the complete range for UAE projects.

Aquasafe Trading stocks Hepworth uPVC drainage pipes from 40mm to 315mm in diameter, covering the full range required for residential and commercial MEP projects. This includes standard sizes of 40mm, 50mm, 75mm, 110mm, 160mm, and 200mm. We also stock the complete range of fittings including elbows, tees, couplers, reducers, and gully traps.

Push-fit and solvent-cement jointing systems should not be mixed within the same drainage run unless specifically designed transition couplings are used. Hepworth supplies separate product ranges for push-fit (ring-seal) and solvent-cement jointing. Our technical team can advise on the correct system selection based on your project specifications and local authority requirements.
