Types of Water Heaters

Storage Water Heaters

Traditional tank-type heaters that store and heat water for immediate use when needed.

  • 30L to 500L capacity
  • Vertical/Horizontal
  • Glass-lined tanks

Instant Water Heaters

Tankless heaters that provide hot water on demand without storing heated water.

  • Compact design
  • Energy saving
  • Unlimited hot water

Solar Water Heaters

Eco-friendly systems that use solar energy to heat water, reducing electricity costs.

  • Flat plate collectors
  • Evacuated tubes
  • Backup heating

Heat Pump Water Heaters

High-efficiency units that use heat pump technology for maximum energy savings.

  • 3x more efficient
  • Low operating cost
  • Eco-friendly

Commercial Water Heaters

High-capacity systems designed for hotels, hospitals, and large commercial facilities.

  • High recovery rate
  • Heavy-duty build
  • Central systems

Accessories & Parts

Common questions about Ariston water heaters and hot water solutions for UAE projects.

We supply Ariston electric storage water heaters, solar thermal water heaters and heat-pump water heaters. Contact us to find the right size and type for your project.

Electric storage heaters are the most common and affordable option with fast installation. Solar water heaters harness UAE's abundant sunlight to significantly reduce electricity bills and are ideal for villas with roof access. Heat-pump water heaters offer the highest energy efficiency (up to 4× more efficient than electric) by extracting heat from ambient air, making them excellent for commercial applications.

Their advanced insulation foam minimises standby heat loss, and energy-saving modes reduce power consumption during off-peak hours.
