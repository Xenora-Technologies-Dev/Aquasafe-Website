Types of Valves

Gate Valves

Linear motion valves used to start or stop fluid flow, ideal for on/off control applications.

  • Full bore design
  • Low pressure drop
  • Rising/Non-rising stem

Ball Valves

Quarter-turn valves with excellent shut-off capabilities for quick operation.

  • Quick shut-off
  • Tight sealing
  • Full/Reduced port

Check Valves

One-way valves that prevent backflow and protect equipment from reverse flow damage.

  • Swing type
  • Spring loaded
  • Wafer type

Butterfly Valves

Compact valves for flow regulation and isolation in large diameter pipelines.

  • Wafer/Lug type
  • Low torque operation
  • Large sizes available

Globe Valves

Linear motion valves designed for precise flow regulation and throttling applications.

  • Excellent throttling
  • Positive shut-off
  • Y-pattern available

Pressure Relief Valves

Safety valves designed to protect systems from overpressure conditions.

  • Automatic operation
  • Adjustable settings
  • Safety certified

Common questions about Vybik brass ball valves and plumbing fittings for MEP projects in UAE.

We supply Vybik brass ball valves, manifold splitters, isolation valves and a full range of plumbing fittings designed for integration with PPR pipe systems. Our product range covers sizes from ½" to 4" to suit residential apartments, commercial buildings and light industrial applications across the UAE.

Ball valves provide quick quarter-turn on/off operation with minimal pressure drop, making them ideal for isolation and shut-off in residential and commercial plumbing. Gate valves offer more precise flow control and are better suited for systems requiring gradual throttling. For most UAE MEP projects, ball valves are preferred due to their durability, low maintenance and reliable sealing performance.
