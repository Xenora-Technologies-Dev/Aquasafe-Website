1. Our Editorial Mission

Aquasafe Trading publishes technical articles, guides, and industry insights on our Blog to help MEP engineers, contractors, procurement specialists, and project managers in the UAE and wider GCC make informed decisions about piping systems, MEP materials, water infrastructure, and related topics.

Our editorial mission is to provide accurate, practical, and up-to-date information rooted in real-world industry experience. We are committed to producing content that reflects genuine expertise in the MEP and piping sector, and that serves the professional needs of our readers.

2. Content Accuracy Commitment

Every article, guide, and resource published on our website undergoes the following quality process before publication:

  • Technical Review: All technical content is reviewed by our in-house MEP specialists with hands-on experience in piping system supply and installation.
  • Fact Verification: Claims relating to product specifications, industry standards, pressure ratings, and material properties are verified against manufacturer datasheets, ISO standards, and DIN norms.
  • Accuracy First: We prioritise factual correctness over publishing speed. Content is not published until it has been reviewed for technical accuracy.
  • No Misleading Claims: We do not publish exaggerated, misleading, or commercially biased claims. Where products from brands we supply are mentioned, this is disclosed.

3. Author Qualifications & Industry Expertise

Content published on the Aquasafe UAE blog is written and reviewed by professionals with direct experience in the following areas:

PP-R & PP-RCT Piping Systems MEP Materials Supply (UAE) Drainage System Engineering Water Pump Selection Pressure Rating & Flow Calculations Construction Material Standards (ISO/DIN) UAE Building Regulations

Our team includes individuals with backgrounds in mechanical engineering, plumbing design, and MEP project coordination with experience spanning UAE infrastructure projects including residential, commercial, and industrial developments.

4. Source Verification

We use credible, verifiable sources to support our content. Our primary sources include:

  • Manufacturer Technical Datasheets: Official product specifications from brands including Polymelt (AGRU), Grundfos, Hepworth, Rheem, and Vybik
  • International Standards: ISO 15874, DIN 8077/8078, EN 1329, and other applicable piping and plumbing standards
  • UAE Regulations: Abu Dhabi Municipality guidelines, Dubai Municipality standards, and UAE building codes
  • Industry Publications: CIBSE, ASHRAE technical documents, and peer-reviewed engineering publications
  • Direct Product Experience: First-hand knowledge gained through years of supplying MEP materials to UAE construction projects

Where we cite external sources in our articles, we link to or reference those sources clearly. We do not present third-party information as our own original research.

5. Independence and Transparency

Aquasafe Trading is an authorised distributor of the brands featured on our website. When our blog articles discuss or compare products we supply, this commercial relationship is acknowledged. Our goal is to provide genuinely useful guidance, not purely promotional content.

  • We do not accept paid guest posts or sponsored articles that present commercial content as neutral editorial
  • Product recommendations are based on technical suitability for the described application, not purely on commercial factors
  • We clearly distinguish between technical guidance and commercial product promotion

6. Update Frequency

We are committed to keeping our content current and relevant:

  • New Articles: We aim to publish new technical articles and guides on a regular basis, covering topics relevant to MEP professionals in the UAE
  • Quarterly Review: Existing articles are reviewed at least quarterly to check for technical accuracy, updated standards, and changes in product availability
  • Immediate Updates: If we become aware of a significant error or a material change (e.g., a standard revision or product recall), we update the relevant content immediately
  • Last Updated Date: Each blog article displays its publication and most recent update date so readers can assess currency

7. Corrections Policy

We take errors seriously and are committed to correcting them promptly and transparently:

  • If a factual error is identified in any published content, we will correct it as quickly as possible
  • Significant corrections are noted within the article with a correction notice indicating what was changed and when
  • We do not silently alter content in a way that misrepresents the original publication without acknowledging the change

If you spot an error, inaccuracy, or outdated information in any of our content, we welcome your feedback. Please contact us using the details below.

8. Scope of Editorial Content

Our editorial content focuses on topics within our area of expertise and direct relevance to our readers in the MEP and construction sectors. Topics covered include:

  • PP-R and PP-RCT piping system selection, installation, and pressure ratings
  • Drainage system design and material selection (uPVC, HDPE, cast iron)
  • Water pump selection criteria and hydraulic calculations
  • Water heater types and selection for UAE climate conditions
  • Valve types, functions, and applications in MEP systems
  • UAE-specific MEP standards, regulations, and best practices
  • Material comparisons and technical guides for procurement professionals

We do not publish content outside our area of expertise. For topics that fall outside our direct knowledge, we clearly indicate the limitations of our perspective and direct readers to authoritative specialist sources.
